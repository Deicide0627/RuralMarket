Our Mission

Our project aims to create a powerful, user-friendly AI tool designed for small farmers, home-based producers, and micro-entrepreneurs in rural Sarawak.

We understand that crafting the perfect product listing can be a barrier. That's why RuralMarket.AI auto-generates compelling product descriptions, suggests competitive pricing, and provides posting guidance—all from a simple product photo and a few details. We want to empower local sellers to reach a wider market and sell their goods more effectively.

Meet the Team

We are a team of students from the University of Technology Sarawak (UTS), studying for our Bachelor in Computer Science. This project is a passion of ours, combining our technical skills with a desire to make a real-world impact in our local community.
